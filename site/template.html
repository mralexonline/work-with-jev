Questions people ask

How long does a typical project take?

A simple website or Facebook page setup is often done within a couple of weeks. Apps and SaaS interfaces take longer, and we'll give you a realistic timeline in your written plan before you commit.

Do I need to know anything technical?

Nope. You tell us about your business, we handle the techy bits and explain things in plain English. When we hand over, you get a short walkthrough so you can make simple changes yourself.

Will I own my website and code?

Yes. Everything we build is handed over to you, including the source code, so you're never trapped with us. We'll keep it in a repository in your name.

Can you work with my existing website?

Absolutely. We can improve, redesign, speed up or move an existing site, and we'll take care to protect the pages and rankings you already have.

Is the AI stuff safe for my customers' data?

We favour open models that can run in a setup you control, we only collect what's needed, and we'll explain clearly where your data goes. If a feature isn't a good fit for your data, we'll say so.

What if I need something that's not on the list?

Ask! The list is a starting point, not a boundary. We'll give you an honest answer on whether it's something we can build well.

Tell us what you need

A couple of lines is plenty. We'll reply with next steps and a rough idea of cost and timing.
